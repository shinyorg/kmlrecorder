Every trip, mapped.
Every engine reading, recorded.

Check in and KML Recorder follows the road - GPS, speed and battery, grouped by trip and exported as KML or GeoJSON. Plug in an OBD-II adapter and it records the engine too: speed, RPM, odometer, temperatures and more, pinned to where you were when you read them.

Built for the road

Record the trip. Record the car.

One tap to check in, and everything from then on is grouped into a trip - ready to drop onto a map.

GPS trips, one tap away

Check in and the app records every reading - latitude, longitude, accuracy, speed and time - until you check out. It keeps going with the screen off, from a foreground service on Android and background location on iOS.

  • Each check-in becomes its own trip - see it on an OpenStreetMap map, export it on its own or with the rest
  • Battery level, charge state and energy saver recorded beside every point
  • Minimum distance and time filters keep the track clean; maximums make sure a long stop still shows up
New

Engine data from any OBD-II adapter

Scan, pick your adapter and connect. KML Recorder reads the vehicle's supported PIDs and its VIN, then streams the parameters you chose - and records them while you drive, tied to your GPS trip.

Bluetooth LEClassic BluetoothWiFi ELM327OBDLink (STN)
  • Reconnects to the last adapter with one tap, protocol pinned so there's no slow search
  • Every sample carries the GPS position it was read at, when you're checked in
  • A PID the car doesn't answer is left out of that sample - never recorded as zero
  • Notices when an adapter stops answering and re-initialises it on its own

Classic Bluetooth adapters (ELM327 v1.5 clones, OBDLink LX) work on Android; iOS uses BLE or WiFi adapters.

New

Pick the PIDs you care about

Start with a sensible default set, then switch on any of 37 parameters - fuel trims, oil temperature, boost, torque, EGR and more. Once you're connected, each one says whether your car supports it.

SpeedRPMOdometerCoolantEngine load ThrottleFuel levelIntake airVoltageMass air flow

on by default

  • Unsupported PIDs are skipped automatically, so the poll stays fast
  • Choose how often to poll, from every second to every five minutes

Export it your way

Trips export as KML or GeoJSON. Engine sessions export as CSV - one row per sample, one column per PID - or as KML with the engine readings attached to every point on the track.

KML 2.2GeoJSONCSV
  • Open an OBD export in Google Earth and tap any point to see what the engine was doing there
  • Deliver to the share sheet, email, Azure Blob Storage or keep it on the device
  • Export one trip or all of them

Home screen widgets

Apple Watch

Battery aware

Charge level, charging state and energy saver are captured with every point, so you can see what a trip cost.

Clean tracks

Minimum and maximum distance and time filters decide which readings are worth keeping.

Stays on your phone

Everything is stored in SQLite on the device. Nothing leaves it until you export.

Reads the VIN and the PIDs your vehicle supports on every connect, so a session is labelled with the car it came from.

Under the hood

The tech stack

C# and .NET 10 from top to bottom - one .NET MAUI code base, native on iOS and Android, with Swift only where Apple requires it.

Language & runtime

  • .NET 10the runtime, AOT compiled on iOS
  • C#the app, services and platform code
  • XAMLthe pages, source generated
  • Swift & SwiftUIthe iOS widget and Apple Watch app

UI & navigation

  • .NET MAUIthe shared UI
  • Mapsui + OpenStreetMapthe trip map - no API key needed
  • Shiny MAUI ShellViewModel-first navigation
  • Shiny Controlstable views, settings cells, feedback
  • CommunityToolkit.Mvvmview models

Location & devices

  • Shiny.Locationsbackground GPS with distance and time filters
  • Shiny.BluetoothLE · Shiny.BluetoothBLE and classic Bluetooth radios
  • Shiny.Hosting.Mauibackground services and lifecycle
